How to play

Infinite Ignition is a portrait arcade game. Tap the left or right side of the screen to steer your rocket through an endless tunnel. Dodge obstacles, collect coins, and spend coins on rocket skins and space backdrops. Scores and progress are saved on your device only — no account required.

Remove Ads purchase

You can buy Remove Ads once in the Skins Shop to turn off interstitial ads. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.
