Infinite Ignition — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This policy describes how Infinite Ignition (“the app”) handles information. The app is published by the developer listed on the App Store.

Summary

  • No account or login is required.
  • Gameplay data (scores, coins, skins, settings) is stored on your device only.
  • The free version may show ads through Google AdMob.
  • Optional in-app purchase: Remove Ads (handled by Apple).

Data stored on your device

The app saves progress locally using iOS storage (UserDefaults), including:

  • High scores and local leaderboard entries
  • Coin balance and owned skins
  • Audio volume and selected backdrop
  • Whether Remove Ads was purchased

This data is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate a backend for Infinite Ignition.

Deleting your data

Delete the app from your device to remove all locally stored game data. Purchase history for Remove Ads is managed by Apple and can be restored with your Apple ID.

Advertising (free version)

If you have not purchased Remove Ads, the app may display interstitial ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect device and usage information to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

On supported iOS versions, the app may ask for permission to use your device identifier for more relevant ads (App Tracking Transparency). You can decline and continue playing.

In-app purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may process purchase-related data according to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Links in the app

The Credits screen may open external websites (for example, OpenGameArt) in Safari when you tap a link. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own systems.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
